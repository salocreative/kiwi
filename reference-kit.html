04Logo and motion

Logo on images

Two options. A soft Kiwi black gradient behind the logo, on a colour or mono image. Or the corner lock-up: the logo in a Kiwi black box, with a border a quarter of the logo's height, locked into a corner.

Motion

Smooth, eased and built on the grid. Things load in, respond to the cursor and settle. Everything stops for people who've set reduced motion.

Rules

How each piece is used, and why.

PieceUseWhy
GridWhere it adds structure: a hero, a feature, a project grid. Most layouts stay cleanUsed sparingly, it stays special
Grid styleThe 144 × 140px module, 1px lines, solid cells, dashed diagonals and guides, Kiwi black or white. One strength per viewIt reads as Kiwi, the logo opened up
Custom gridsBuilt for the layout from the same module, lines and diagonals, fitted around the contentEach layout gets the grid it needs, in one system
HeroA mono photo with colour windows, the fading grid, one headline, one sentence, one buttonAnswers "why us?" before anyone scrolls
Text and imageHalf photo in colour, half copy, the ambient grid on the copy sideSay less, show more
Project gridProject photos in colour on the structure grid, a location label on eachThe work does the talking
Image treatmentsStandard colour for projects, mono for atmosphere, the colour window once per pageClients see the real finish
Text treatmentsOne lead move per layout, with at most one supporting move. Marker for a figure or date, block for wordsBold, not busy
Logo on imagesA soft Kiwi black gradient behind it, or the corner lock-upThe logo stays clear on any photo
AccentSmall doses: highlights, eyebrows, the accent button, accent icons, with Kiwi black textMono with one highlight
IconsMaterial Symbols Sharp, weight 300, with a label. Kiwi black, white or the accentSquare and technical, like the logo
LabelsCapitals for short labels. Sentence case for headings, navigation and buttonsEasier to read
ButtonsSquare, 1px border and the arrow boxPrecise, like the logo
Poster and collateralThe logo big, one line, wearekiwi.co.ukRead from across a room or a street
MotionEased, smooth and on the grid. Load in, respond to the cursor, settlePremium, not showy
CornersSquare everywhereSquare corners throughout
