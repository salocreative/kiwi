Writing style

A few simple rules so everyone at Kiwi writes the same way.

UK English

colour, programme, organise

Write “Kiwi” in sentences

Not KIWI or Kiwi Design. KIWI is for the logo only. Kiwi Design is for legal text only.

Short sentences

One idea per sentence. Cut any word that doesn’t add something.

“We” and “you”

We’ll manage the programme. Not: Kiwi will manage the client’s programme.

Active voice

We fitted out the store. Not: The store was fitted out by our team.

Sentence case headings

Our work. Not: Our Work.

Numbers

Use numerals: 3 sites, 40 years. Spell out a number only at the start of a sentence.

Dates and times

12 October 2026. 9am, 5.30pm.

Phone numbers

01202 501745

Fit-out

Fit-out as a noun, fit out as a verb. We fit out stores. The fit-out took a month.

Punctuation

No exclamation marks. Write “and”, not “&”, in sentences.

Plain words

Say what you mean. Avoid the words below.

Words to avoid

  • Solutions
  • Turnkey
  • World-class
  • Best-in-class
  • Cutting-edge
  • Seamless
  • Synergy
  • Leverage
  • Passionate about

Say this, not that

Say

We make it happen.

Not

We pride ourselves on delivering exceptional outcomes for our valued clients.

Say

One team from first drawing to handover.

Not

An end-to-end turnkey fit-out solution.

Say

Talk to one of our directors.

Not

Please do not hesitate to contact a member of our team.

04Website

Grid

Built from the logo’s construction lines. A 144 × 140px module at 1440 × 840, in 1px lines: solid for cells, dashed for diagonals and guides. Use it where it adds structure, such as a hero, a feature or a project grid. Most layouts stay clean.

Feature · 100%

The grid is the graphic.

Structure · 35%

Over photos and dark layouts.

Ambient · 15%

Behind copy.

On a photo

Structure strength in white, over a darkened photo.

One strength per view

Feature, structure or ambient. Never mix them in one layout.

Kiwi black or white

Lines are 1px. Diagonals share the colour and weight of the grid.

Built for the layout

Custom grids use the same module, fitted around the content.

Per section, with at most one supporting motion.

Scroll stays scroll

No pinned or hijacked scrolling. Hover effects are extras, never the only way to see something.

Respect reduced motion

Show the final state. Anything that moves for more than 5 seconds gets a pause control.

Website rules

The basics every page follows.

Square corners

Everywhere. No rounded buttons, cards or images.

Sentence case

Headings, navigation and buttons. Capitals only for short labels and eyebrows.

Say less, show more

One headline, one sentence and one button in a hero.

Real content

Only claims, figures and names Kiwi have confirmed. Anything else is marked [TBC].

Icons with words

Material Symbols Sharp, weight 300, with a label. Kiwi black, white or the accent.

Readable for everyone

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and controls, visible focus, works with a keyboard.

01Email signature

Fill in your details, copy the signature and paste it into your email app.

Hi Sam,

Drawings attached for review.

Thanks,

  1. Fill in your details.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. Paste it into your email app. Steps for each app are on the right.
  4. Send yourself a test email.
  1. Go to Settings › Accounts › Signatures.
  2. Select New signature, name it Kiwi and paste with Ctrl + V. Select Save.
  3. Under Select default signatures, choose Kiwi for new messages and replies. Select Save.
  1. Start a new email and go to Message › Signature › Signatures.
  2. Select New, name it Kiwi, then paste into Edit signature with Ctrl + V.
  3. Set New messages and Replies/forwards to Kiwi. Select OK.
  1. Go to Outlook › Settings › Signatures.
  2. Select +, name it Kiwi, paste with Cmd + V and save.
  3. Under Choose default signature, set new messages and replies to Kiwi.
  1. Go to Settings › Account › Signatures.
  2. Select New signature, name it Kiwi and paste.
  3. Choose Kiwi for new messages and replies. Select Save.
  1. Go to Settings › See all settings › General › Signature.
  2. Select Create new, name it Kiwi and paste.
  3. Set Signature defaults to Kiwi, then Save Changes at the bottom.
  1. In Mail, go to Mail › Settings › Signatures.
  2. Click your account on the left, then +. Name it Kiwi and paste with Cmd + V.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, so the signature keeps its own fonts.
  4. Set Choose Signature to Kiwi, then close the window.
  1. On your computer, send yourself an email with your Kiwi signature in it.
  2. On your iPhone or iPad, open that email, press and hold on the signature, and copy it.
  3. Go to Settings › Apps › Mail › Signature (on older versions, Settings › Mail › Signature) and paste.
  4. If the formatting turns plain, shake the device and tap Undo. Phone signatures are simpler, so small differences are normal.
Something not right?
Pasted as plain text

Copy again and paste with Ctrl + V or Cmd + V, not “paste as plain text”.

Logo missing

Your email app may be hiding images. Select “Show images” in your test email.

Logo arrives as an attachment

Classic Outlook sometimes does this. Ask IT to switch off “send pictures with document”.

Details changed

Update them here, copy again and paste over the old signature.
