How it works

Five steps, one team.

  1. 01Brief
  2. 02Design
  3. 03Plan
  4. 04Build
  5. 05Handover
  1. 01

    Step 1: Brief

    We survey your site, learn your brand and agree the opening date.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Design

    We draw it up, choose materials and finishes, and handle planning and Building Regulations applications where you need them.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Plan

    We set one programme, with a date for every trade, working back from your opening day.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Build

    We build it with our own specialists, and keep the site safe and tidy while we work.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Handover

    We hand it over finished and ready for your opening day.

Since 1986

Forty years, start to finish

1986

  1. 1986

    Kiwi Design Limited is incorporated on 27 November.

  2. 1993

    Our own joinery workshop opens, so more of each job is made under one roof.

  3. 2001

    Our first national retail roll-out: the same store, fitted out in towns across the UK.

  4. 2008

    We move to Bessemer Close in Verwood, with room for the workshop and the team to grow.

  5. 2013

    Our first gym fit-out, and the start of our work in leisure.

  6. 2017

    We extend and fit out JD's North of England flagship at Liverpool ONE: 40,000 sq ft over 2 floors.

  7. 2018

    We run 3 JD flagship fit-outs at once, in Leeds, Birmingham and Bluewater.

  8. 2018

    We fit out Footpatrol in Soho, and the store reopens after about 5 weeks.

  9. 2023

    We start working with Under Armour, from Oxford Street to Gunwharf Quays.

  10. 2026

    Forty years of making it happen.

About Kiwi

Our people are the difference.

We started as a family-run business. Today Kiwi is owned by its people, through an Employee Ownership Trust, so the team on your project has a stake in it. Our home is Verwood, Dorset, and we work on sites across the UK, from Oxford Street to Banbridge. Many of our clients come back for the next one.
